KomiTrail: Learn Go — Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026. Published by Brandon Enterprises. Contact: brich33713@gmail.com.

This policy describes KomiTrail version 0.1.0 while Pro purchases are unavailable. It will be revised before a build that enables purchases is released.

Learning and play data

KomiTrail stores saved games, lesson progress, puzzle attempts, activity dates, and preferences on your device. You do not need an account. This version does not send learning or play data to Brandon Enterprises and does not include ads or developer analytics.

Backups

If you export a backup, KomiTrail writes an unencrypted JSON file containing your progress and settings to the location you choose. If you choose a cloud-backed file provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms. Your device or operating system may also offer backup or transfer features. Keep exported files somewhere you trust.

Purchases

Pro purchases and restoration are unavailable in this version. A future version may offer a one-time purchase through Google Play, with purchase verification through a publisher-operated service. Before that version is released, this policy and the Play Data safety declaration will be updated to explain the information transmitted and retained.

Removing your data

You can erase local app data in Android settings or uninstall KomiTrail. Delete exported files and any copies held by other services separately.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to brich33713@gmail.com.
